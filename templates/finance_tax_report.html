📘 User Guide — How to Use the {% if profile.is_vat %}GCC VAT{% else %}GST{% endif %} Dashboard
{% if profile.is_vat %}

GCC VAT Principles (UAE FTA / Saudi ZATCA):

  • Standard Rated Supplies: Supplies of goods and services subject to standard VAT (5% in UAE & Oman, 15% in Saudi Arabia, 10% in Bahrain).
  • Zero-Rated Supplies (0%): Exports of goods/services outside the GCC, international transport, and designated healthcare/education. Full input VAT is recoverable on zero-rated supplies.
  • Exempt Supplies: Financial services, local passenger transport, residential property leases. No output VAT is charged, and input VAT incurred on exempt supplies is non-recoverable.
  • Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): On imported goods or services from outside the country, the recipient accounts for both output VAT and recoverable input VAT in the same return period.
  • Net VAT Due: Box 6 represents your final liability to the tax authority (Federal Tax Authority in UAE, ZATCA in Saudi Arabia).
{% else %}

India GST Principles (CGST, SGST, IGST):

  • Intra-State Supplies: When supplier and place of supply are in the same State, CGST (Central Tax) and SGST (State Tax) are charged equally (e.g. 9% + 9% = 18%).
  • Inter-State Supplies: When supplier and place of supply are in different States, or on imports into India, IGST (Integrated Tax) is charged in full (18%).
  • Input Tax Credit (ITC) Utilization Order: IGST credit is utilized first against IGST liability, then CGST, then SGST. CGST credit cannot be set off against SGST liability, and vice versa.
  • HSN / SAC Reporting: Required for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings, summarizing item sales by Harmonized System of Nomenclature code.
{% endif %}
