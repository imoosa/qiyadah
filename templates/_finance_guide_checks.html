📘 User Guide — What is Accounting Checks?

In simple words: this page checks if your invoices, receipts, payments and bank records agree with your accounts. It only shows problems. It never changes or fixes anything.

How to use it:

  1. Look at Check status at the top. “No exceptions found” means all is fine for what was checked.
  2. If it says Review required, scroll down to the list of review items.
  3. Click an item to open the original record.
  4. Correct the record on its own screen, then come back here to confirm the item is gone.

What it looks for: unposted or unbalanced journal entries, invoices with no accounting entry, receipts/payments that do not match their entry, opening balance differences, and bank balance differences.

Good to know: a flagged item is something to review, not always a mistake. A clean result is not an audit. Run this weekly and before closing a month in Financial Periods.
