Official 3-step workflow to generate and file your e-invoices and e-way bills

Compliance Guide
1
Choose & review invoice

Select your approved sales tax invoice. Line items, HSN/SAC codes, and GST values are auto-populated.

2
Complete details & download JSON

Confirm party GSTINs, Place of Supply, and optional road transport particulars (Vehicle & Distance).

3
Upload on government portal

Upload the downloaded JSON directly to NIC or IRP to receive your official IRN, QR code and EWB number.

ℹ️ Legal Notice: Downloading a file does not generate an IRN, signed QR code or e-way bill number. The government portal validates eligibility, GSTIN/HSN masters, reporting deadlines and the uploaded data upon submission.
