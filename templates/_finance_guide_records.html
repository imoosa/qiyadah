📘 User Guide — What are Financial Records?

In simple words: this is the list of source documents behind your accounts — invoices, receipts, payments, credit/debit notes, ageing and tax. It shows what was entered.

How to use it:

  1. Pick a category from the Finance menu (for example Sales Invoices or Receivable Ageing).
  2. Use the filters (dates, due status, ageing bucket) to narrow the list.
  3. Open a row to see its details.

Next step: to test whether these records agree with your accounts, open Accounting Checks.
