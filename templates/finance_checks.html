How these checks work

Account openings are checked for debit/credit balance. Bank balances use openings plus recorded movements, counting recognised opening transactions only once. Receipt/payment journals are compared with source amounts, dates and accounts.

Zero means no records were checked in that category; it does not confirm a reconciled balance. Coverage depends on available records and your permissions.
